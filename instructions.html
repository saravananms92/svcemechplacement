Placement Drive – Instructions

  • Students must strictly adhere to the schedule communicated by the Training & Placement Cell.
  • Reporting late to the venue may result in disqualification.
  • College identity card and required documents must be produced upon request.
  • Students should always bring THREE passport size photographs.
  • Aadhaar Card and PAN Card are mandatory.
  • Formal dress code and professional conduct are mandatory throughout the process.
  • Students must not indulge in any activity that may affect the integrity of the placement process.
  • Decisions taken by the company and the Placement Cell shall be final and binding.
